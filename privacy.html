Privacy

How this website handles your information.

Contact details and event enquiries

Opera Lux Events, operated by Derek Rue, uses the details you provide to respond to your event enquiry and discuss a possible booking. Contact: agency@operaluxevents.com.

The form asks for your name, email, event type and location. A company name, event date, guest count and message are optional. Please do not include sensitive personal information. Enquiry details are not a newsletter subscription.

Form processing

Form submissions are sent to Formspree, an external form processing service. If you prefer, email Derek directly. Read Formspree’s privacy policy.

Hosting and video

The site is prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. Hosting providers may process technical request data, including IP addresses. GitHub’s privacy statement describes its processing.

Fonts and photographs are hosted with the site. The featured YouTube player loads only when you select play. Loading it connects to YouTube; its privacy practices then apply. Google’s privacy policy. The Porta Nigra performance video is hosted with the site.

Questions about your details

Contact agency@operaluxevents.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of information supplied with an enquiry.
