Derek Rue · Solo Singer, Theater Trier

Derek grew up in Los Angeles, California, and trained at the Universität Mozarteum Salzburg under Christoph Stehl. He was an ensemble member at the Mittelsächsisches Theater from 2015 to 2018 and joined Theater Trier in 2019.

His stage work includes Fenton in Falstaff, Alfred in Die Fledermaus, Jack Twist in Brokeback Mountain and Ferrando in Così fan tutte. Opera Lux Events brings that professional singing experience to company dinners, galas, receptions and private celebrations.

You plan the programme directly with Derek: the songs, the timing and how the performance fits your venue. Solo event performances combine his live voice with recorded orchestral backing. Duets with a soprano can be discussed, subject to availability.

Based in the Moselle region, Derek offers performances in Luxembourg and Germany. Send the venue or town when you enquire so travel and practical details can be agreed.
