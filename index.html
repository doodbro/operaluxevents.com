About

Derek is a solo singer at Theater Trier and a graduate of the Universität Mozarteum Salzburg, with fifteen years on professional stages. But when you book Opera Lux, the credentials aren't the point. What your guests get is a live performance, in the room with them.

Derek plans the set personally with the host, song by song, around the venue and the evening. On the night he reads the room: when to stay soft while people talk, when to open up the full voice. Every note live. Songs people actually know.

Fair Questions

What event planners
ask before booking.

Do our guests need to know opera?

No. Derek can combine opera with musical theatre and crossover songs. You choose the balance together, based on the guests and the occasion.

What does the venue need to provide?

A stage is not essential. Derek brings equipment and orchestral accompaniment. Space, power, access and sound requirements are agreed with the venue before booking.

Can it fit around speeches and dinner service?

Yes. A featured set or several shorter sets can be planned around your running order. Share your schedule when you enquire so Derek can suggest the right format.

Does Derek perform in Luxembourg?

Opera Lux serves Luxembourg and Germany. Send the venue or town with your enquiry so travel and practical details can be discussed.

What happens after an enquiry?

Derek checks the date and discusses the programme and practical requirements with you. The performance and booking terms are agreed before you commit. An enquiry does not reserve a date.
